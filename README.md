## English

I build software under the atomiXbilişim name. Most of it is business software for small and mid-sized companies: work orders for repair shops, appointment booking, rentals, POS screens, engineering paperwork. I usually take a project from the first conversation with the customer all the way to the live server, and I keep maintaining it after that.

Most of my clients are in Türkiye, but I've also worked on projects for companies in Switzerland and Germany.

My products are sold under commercial licenses, so the code itself lives in private repos. This is what I've been working on.

### Live

- [AtomixServis](https://github.com/atakanozdal/atomixservis): management software for auto repair shops. Work orders, vehicle intake, QR labels, stock. Web panel plus iOS and Android apps, [on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/tr/app/atomixservis/id6831888179).
- atomiX Health Booth: queue, incident records and branch stock for nursing booths. Running at a customer site.
- atomiX Engineering: quotes, jobs, site work and progress payments for engineering offices. Demo available.

### In progress

- AtomixServis editions for motorcycle, boiler and air-conditioning service shops
- atomiX TeknikServis, a multi-tenant system for computer repair shops
- Bİ-Kİ, bicycle rental management with its own mobile app
- atomiX Restaurant: POS, kitchen display and QR ordering
- Booking and client records for physiotherapy clinics, nail studios and hair salons
- atomiX Hidro, hydraulic calculations and drawings for rural drinking water projects
- atomiX SEO, website audits with PDF reports
- atomiXmail, my own webmail client
- A license server that all of the above use
- A small Windows tool for loading configs onto Cisco C927 routers

### Open source

- [mail2sms-atomix](https://github.com/atakanozdal/mail2sms-atomix): a WordPress plugin that turns outgoing notification emails into SMS through Netgsm
- [amelia-netgsm-atomix](https://github.com/atakanozdal/amelia-netgsm-atomix): Netgsm SMS for the Amelia booking plugin
- [amelia-netgsm-atomix-webhook](https://github.com/atakanozdal/amelia-netgsm-atomix-webhook): a small token-protected endpoint that sends SMS through Netgsm

### What I use

PHP, MySQL and SQLite on the server, plain JavaScript in the browser, Capacitor for mobile, C# and .NET for Windows tools, Python when the math gets heavy. WordPress when the client needs it.

## Türkçe

Bir projeyi genelde müşteriyle ilk görüşmeden canlı sunucuya kadar kendim götürüyorum, sonrasında da bakımını yapıyorum.

Müşterilerimin çoğu Türkiye'de, ama İsviçre ve Almanya'daki firmalar için de iş yaptım.
